Post Matric Scholarship Scheme for Minorities

Offered by: Ministry of Minority Affairs

Official Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in

Scholarship Benefits

Admission & tuition fee waiver up to ₹10,000/year plus maintenance allowance up to ₹1,200/month

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Minimum 50% marks or equivalent grade in previous final examination

Income Criteria: Annual family income ≤ ₹2,00,000 from all sources

Category: Minority (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi)

Domicile: All India
