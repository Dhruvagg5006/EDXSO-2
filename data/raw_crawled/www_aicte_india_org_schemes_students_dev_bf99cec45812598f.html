AICTE Saksham Scholarship Scheme for Specially-Abled Students

Offered by: All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)

Official Portal: https://www.aicte-india.org/schemes/students-development-schemes

Scholarship Benefits

₹50,000 per annum for every year of study towards fees and assistive technologies

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Specially abled students having disability not less than 40% admitted to technical degree/diploma

Income Criteria: Family income ≤ ₹8,00,000 per annum

Category: Persons with Disabilities (PwD ≥ 40%)

Domicile: All India

Last Date / Closing Date: 2026-10-31
