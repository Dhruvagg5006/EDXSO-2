Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF)

Offered by: Ministry of Education, Govt of India

Official Portal: https://www.pmrf.in

Scholarship Benefits

₹70,000/mo (Y1-2), ₹75,000/mo (Y3), ₹80,000/mo (Y4-5) plus ₹2,00,000 annual contingency research grant

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Enrolled in Ph.D. program in science/tech at PMRF granting institutes with qualifying CGPA ≥ 8.0 or GATE score ≥ 650

Income Criteria: Not specified

Category: All / General

Domicile: All India
