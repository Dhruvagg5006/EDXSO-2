Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP) Post-Matric Scheme

Offered by: Social Welfare Department, Govt of Karnataka

Official Portal: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in

Scholarship Benefits

Full reimbursement of college tuition fees plus monthly maintenance stipend

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Enrolled in post-matriculation diploma, UG, or PG degree programs in Karnataka

Income Criteria: Family income ≤ ₹2,50,000 (SC/ST) or ≤ ₹1,00,000 (Cat-1/OBC)

Category: SC, ST, Category-1, OBC

Domicile: Karnataka

Application Timeline

Opening Date: 2026-07-20

Last Date / Closing Date: 2026-11-15
