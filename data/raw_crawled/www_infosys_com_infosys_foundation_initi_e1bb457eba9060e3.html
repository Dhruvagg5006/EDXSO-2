Infosys Foundation STEM Stars Scholarship for Girls

Offered by: Infosys Foundation

Official Portal: https://www.infosys.com/infosys-foundation/initiatives/education/stem-stars.html

Scholarship Benefits

Up to ₹1,00,000 per year covering tuition fees, living expenses, books, and laptop for 4-year B.Tech

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: First-year female students enrolled in engineering (STEM) courses at NIRF top-ranked institutes

Income Criteria: Annual family income ≤ ₹8,00,000 per annum

Category: All / General

Domicile: All India
