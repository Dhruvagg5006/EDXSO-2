Prime Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme (PMSSS) for J&K and Ladakh

Offered by: AICTE, Ministry of Education

Official Portal: https://www.aicte-india.org/bureaus/pmsss

Scholarship Benefits

Academic fee up to ₹3,00,000 for Engineering plus ₹1,00,000 annual maintenance allowance

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Domicile of UTs of J&K and Ladakh who have passed 10+2 examination from JKBOSE or CBSE affiliated schools in J&K/Ladakh

Income Criteria: Family income ≤ ₹8,00,000 per annum

Category: All / General (Domicile specific)

Domicile: Jammu & Kashmir / Ladakh

Application Timeline

Opening Date: 2026-07-01

Last Date / Closing Date: 2026-10-20
