Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (SVMCM)

Offered by: Department of Higher Education, Govt of West Bengal

Official Portal: https://svmcm.wbhed.gov.in

Scholarship Benefits

₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month (₹12,000 to ₹60,000 annually) based on course category

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Minimum 60% marks in aggregate in qualifying Madhyamik / Higher Secondary exam

Income Criteria: Total annual family income ≤ ₹2,50,000 per annum

Category: All / General

Domicile: West Bengal

Application Timeline

Opening Date: 2026-08-01

Last Date / Closing Date: 2026-10-31
