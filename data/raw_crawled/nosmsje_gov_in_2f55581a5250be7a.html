National Overseas Scholarship for SC/ST Candidates

Offered by: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

Official Portal: https://nosmsje.gov.in

Scholarship Benefits

Full tuition fee coverage, living allowance of USD 15,400 (or GBP 9,900), medical insurance and airfare

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Minimum 60% marks in qualifying degree for Master's or Ph.D. abroad in top 500 QS world-ranked universities

Income Criteria: Total family income ≤ ₹8,00,000 per annum

Category: Scheduled Castes (SC), Denotified Nomadic Tribes

Domicile: All India
