DST INSPIRE Scholarship for Higher Education (SHE)

Offered by: Department of Science & Technology, Ministry of Science & Technology

Official Portal: https://online-inspire.gov.in

Scholarship Benefits

₹80,000 per annum (₹60,000 annual scholarship + ₹20,000 mentorship project grant)

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Top 1% in Class 12 board examination or rank within top 10,000 in JEE/NEET, pursuing B.Sc / Int. M.Sc in Basic & Natural Sciences

Income Criteria: Not specified

Category: All / General
