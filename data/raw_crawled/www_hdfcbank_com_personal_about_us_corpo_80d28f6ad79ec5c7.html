Scholarship Benefits

Up to ₹75,000 for postgraduate/professional studies and up to ₹35,000 for school/UG students

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Students from Class 1 to PG level, must have passed previous exam with minimum 55% marks

Income Criteria: Annual family income ≤ ₹2,50,000 or families facing acute financial crisis (loss of breadwinner/medical distress)
