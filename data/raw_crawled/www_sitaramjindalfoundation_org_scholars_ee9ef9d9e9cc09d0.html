Sitaram Jindal Foundation Scholarship Scheme

Offered by: Sitaram Jindal Foundation

Official Portal: https://www.sitaramjindalfoundation.org/scholarships.php

Scholarship Benefits

₹500 to ₹3,200 per month depending on degree level (ITI to PG / Medical / Engineering)

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Students with minimum 60% marks (Boys) or 55% marks (Girls) in previous qualifying examination

Income Criteria: Family income ≤ ₹4,00,000 per annum (for employed parents) or ≤ ₹2,50,000 (for others)

Category: All / General

Domicile: All India

Application Timeline

Opening Date: 2026-01-01
