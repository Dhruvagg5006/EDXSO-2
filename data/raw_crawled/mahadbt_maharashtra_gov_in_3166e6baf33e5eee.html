Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna

Offered by: Directorate of Higher Education, Govt of Maharashtra

Official Portal: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

Scholarship Benefits

50% tuition fees and 50% exam fees waiver for approved professional/non-professional courses

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements: Admitted through Centralized Admission Process (CAP) in recognized higher education courses

Income Criteria: Annual family income ≤ ₹8,00,000 per annum

Category: Open / General / SEBC (Without Caste-based Free-ship)

Domicile: Maharashtra

Application Timeline

Opening Date: 2026-08-01

Last Date / Closing Date: 2026-11-30
